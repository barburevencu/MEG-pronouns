# Image prompts for the place nouns

One prompt per image, pasted whole into a new ChatGPT chat. No reference image.
Square 1:1, as for the people; resize to 1254 × 1254 afterwards if needed.

Every place is shown empty, from the inside, in ordinary condition (the place
adjectives include vide, vaste, luxueux, délabré, insalubre, so no image should
look like any of those). Its most recognisable feature sits in the centre of the
frame, where the eyes are, instead of an empty aisle or floor. No human or animal
figures appear anywhere, not even in artworks, so no place carries a gender cue;
for that reason the French insignia are tricolour flags, not Marianne. No text
on this first pass.
